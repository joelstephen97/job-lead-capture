LeadingLeads — Options
Choose how leads are captured and stored locally.
Keyword terms
One term per line. Matching is case-insensitive and uses “contains”. If empty, everything matches.
Grant optional permission to auto-scan any job board or website for leads.
How scanning works
  • Click the extension → “Scan this page”.
  • On job search pages, it tries to read each job card and match your terms.
  • If no cards are found, it falls back to scanning the page text for emails.
This extension reads only what is rendered in your browser and stores leads locally in your Chrome profile. It does not send scraped data to any server. You are responsible for using it in compliance with each site’s Terms of Service.
For convenience, the extension stores a short context snippet from the card/page where the email was found so you can understand why it was captured.
Privacy & Terms
Please read this carefully — it’s designed to keep you informed and in control:
  • Local-only storage: your leads are saved to your Chrome profile via chrome.storage.local.
  • No data sent out: LeadingLeads does not upload leads to any server or third-party service.
  • On-device scanning: it reads the rendered page content in your browser; nothing is scraped server-side.
  • Site Terms: you are responsible for using this extension in compliance with each website’s Terms of Service.
